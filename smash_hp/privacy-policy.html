Privacy Policy for Smash HP

Effective Date: 21 May 2026

Smash HP ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how Smash HP handles information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Smash HP does not require account registration and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

Local Data Storage

Smash HP stores gameplay preferences locally on your device, including:

  • Player profile settings
  • Character selection
  • Battle session progress
  • Sound and vibration preferences

This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Advertising

Smash HP uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob may collect certain device information and usage data to provide and improve advertising services, including:

  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • App interaction data
  • Approximate diagnostics information

Please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy for more information:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Third-Party Services

Smash HP uses:

  • Google AdMob

These third-party services may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Children’s Privacy

Smash HP is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

Because Smash HP does not store user data on external servers, gameplay information remains stored locally on your device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
